Frequently Asked Questions

Find answers to common queries about our artwork process, delivery in Hyderabad and across India, and services.

How do I place an order?

Simply fill out our contact form, send us a WhatsApp message, or call us directly. Share your photo and requirements, and we will guide you through the rest of the process.

How long does it take to create artwork?

Timelines depend on the complexity and type of artwork. Basic sketches take 5-7 business days, standard portraits take 3-5 days, and premium canvas pieces take 2-4 days. We always provide an estimated timeline upfront.

Do you offer revisions?

Yes! We want you to love your artwork. Our packages include revisions (1 for Basic, 2 for Standard, unlimited for Premium). We share a digital preview and make adjustments until you are fully satisfied.

What payment methods do you accept?

We accept cash on delivery (Hyderabad local customers), UPI payments (Google Pay, PhonePe, Paytm), and bank transfers. Full payment is required for digital deliveries, while physical prints require a 50% advance. Special discounts available for Hyderabad bulk orders and corporate gifting.

Do you deliver outside Hyderabad?

Yes, we ship artwork across India and internationally. Hyderabad local customers can opt for free pickup or cash-on-delivery. For outstation deliveries, shipping charges may apply depending on your location and the size of the artwork.

What photo quality do you need?

High-resolution photos with good lighting produce the best results. We accept JPG, PNG, and HEIC formats. If you are unsure about your photo quality, send it to us and we will advise.
